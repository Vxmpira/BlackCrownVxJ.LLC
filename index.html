How we work.

Four rules every BlackCrown product is held to.

Built by the desk that uses it

These tools come off a working trading and building desk. If something is in the product, it is because it earned its place in a real session.

Information, never signals

We tell you what happened, how much it matters and what to watch. We do not tell you to buy or sell, and we do not promise results.

Your data stays yours

Nothing you write, track or upload in a BlackCrown product is sold or used to train a model. That is the default, not a setting.

Try it before you pay

Korvus, Crown and Eclipse-X are free to start with no card. Paid plans cancel anytime, and LodeStone on Tradovate is a single purchase.

Questions, answered straight.

Not answered here? Ask the desk.

Do the products work on their own?

Yes. Korvus, LodeStone, Crown and Eclipse-X each stand alone, and none of them requires another. They are built so that using more than one adds up, not so that one is useless without the rest.

Where should I start?

Use the fit finder above. In short: if you trade, open a free Observer account on Korvus. If you want levels on your chart, look at LodeStone. If you want a private AI, Crown is free to start with no card. Eclipse-X is open to everyone.

What does it all cost?

Korvus is free as an Observer, or $9.99 a month ($79.99 a year) as an Operator. LodeStone is $149 once on Tradovate or $19.99 a month on TradingView, and it is included with Korvus Operator. Crown is free to start, or $49.99 a month for Crowned. Eclipse-X is free.

What happens to my data?

Nothing you write, track or upload in a BlackCrown product is sold or used to train a model. The details are in the privacy policy.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. Our tools give information and context. They do not place trades, give signals or predict outcomes, and trading carries a substantial risk of loss. See the disclaimer.

How do I get support?

The support desk routes billing, bugs and product questions to the right place. The product rooms in Eclipse-X usually answer fastest, and email reaches the desk directly at BlackCrownVxJ.LLC@outlook.com.
