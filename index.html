Questions

Straight answers.

Do the products work on their own?+

Yes. Korvus, LodeStone, Crown and Eclipse-X are each built to stand alone, and none of them requires another. They are designed so that running more than one compounds the edge, not so that one is useless without the rest.

Where should I start?+

If you trade, open a free Observer account on korvus.industries and see the scored feed for yourself. If you want the chart side, LodeStone is a one-time purchase on Tradovate or comes included with Korvus Operator. If you want a private AI, Crown is free to start with no card. Eclipse-X, the house Discord, is open to everyone.

What happens to my data?+

Nothing you write, track or upload in a BlackCrown product is sold or used to train a model. Privacy is the default across the house, not a setting you go hunting for.

How do I get support?+

The support desk routes billing, bugs and product questions to the right place. The product rooms in Eclipse-X, the house Discord, usually answer fastest, and email reaches the desk directly at BlackCrownVxJ.LLC@outlook.com.
