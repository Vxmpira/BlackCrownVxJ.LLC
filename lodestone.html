What it draws.

One indicator carries the whole framework, labeled at the right edge and kept current.

Session highs and lows

Completed Asia, London, NY AM and NY PM highs and lows, plus prior day and prior week. Each level runs past the live bar with its name at the line, so you always know which pool of liquidity you are looking at.

The dealing range

Premium and discount, built from confirmed swing points, a completed session or the prior day, with an equilibrium line and subtle shading. One glance tells you which half of the range you are paying in.

Resting liquidity

Relative equal highs and lows are marked where they form. Extra equal swings reinforce the existing line instead of stacking duplicates.

SMT divergence

Your chart's swings compared against a correlated symbol, with automatic NQ and ES pairing and a manual override. A flag is stamped only when the swing confirms.

A restrained volume profile

No histogram. Only the finished levels print: naked points of control that remove themselves when price trades at them, or point of control with value area high and low.

Labels that never overlap

Names sit at a shared right edge. Levels that sit close together merge into one stacked label, each row carrying its exact price, so the chart stays readable at any zoom.

A chart that cleans itself

Swept levels fade or remove themselves and old levels age out. The map stays current instead of stacking clutter.

Is LodeStone right for you?

It is a map, not a signal service.

A good fit if

  • You trade NQ, ES or YM, or the micros that track them
  • You already use session levels, prior highs and lows, or ICT concepts
  • You chart on Tradovate or TradingView
  • You want the drawing done so you can focus on the decision

Probably not for you if

  • You want arrows that tell you when to buy or sell. LodeStone never draws one
  • You trade markets outside index futures. The logic is tuned to the indexes
  • You expect an indicator to guarantee an outcome. None can

Questions.

Not answered here? Ask the desk.

How does delivery work on Tradovate?

After checkout the indicator file ships to the email you used at checkout. Load it into Tradovate and the map is on your chart. One purchase, yours for good, nothing to cancel.

How does access work on TradingView?

LodeStone is an invite-only script on TradingView, so access is granted to your exact TradingView username. If you subscribe here, the desk handles it with you in the #korvus-indicator channel on Discord. If you are a Korvus Operator, save your TradingView username on your Korvus account page and access is granted from there, normally within 24 hours. It then appears under Invite-only scripts in your Indicators panel.

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

No. A free TradingView account can run LodeStone once access is granted to your username.

What markets is it built for?

Index futures first: NQ, ES and YM, and the micros that track them. The session map, value logic and SMT comparisons are tuned to how the indexes trade.

Can I control what it draws?

Yes. Every module has its own switch and the inputs are yours: session windows and timezone, swing strength, tolerances, value area percent, colors, labels and how swept levels are cleaned up.

Does it repaint?

No. Every level, swing and profile updates on confirmed bars only. Nothing prints and then quietly disappears after the fact. What you screenshot is what happened.
