Under the hood

Your rules, your levels.

LodeStone maps the framework, but you decide how it trades with you. Every module has a switch and every level has a dial.

  • Sessions on your clock. Set the timezone and the exact windows for Asia, London, NY AM and NY PM, each with its own color.
  • Draw only what you trade. Prior levels, premium and discount, resting liquidity, SMT and the volume profile all toggle independently.
  • Tune the signal. Swing strength, equal-high tolerance, value area percent, label size and extension are all yours to set.
  • Nothing repaints. Levels and flags print and stay. What you see in review is what you would have seen live.
  • A self-cleaning chart. When price sweeps a level, LodeStone removes or fades it, so the map stays current instead of stacking clutter.
