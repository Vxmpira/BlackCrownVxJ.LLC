House rules

How the floor stays clean.

Four rules, enforced by culture more than moderation. They are why the room is worth being in.

Rule 01

Signal over noise

No engagement bait, no clout chasing, no spam. If it does not help someone act, it belongs somewhere else.

Rule 02

Scoped to the work

The floor talks tape, tools and building. Off-topic drift gets steered back without drama.

Rule 03

Attack ideas, not people

Pressure-testing a read is the point. Disrespecting the person holding it is not.

Rule 04

Questions asked in the open

The desk answers where everyone can see, so one person's question becomes the whole floor's answer.

Questions

Before you walk in.

Is it really free?+

Yes. The invite is open, there is no fee, and no card is involved. Eclipse-X is the front porch of the house, not a product.

Do I need to own a product to join?+

No. Plenty of people walk in before they buy anything, and some never buy at all. The floor is open either way.

Where does LodeStone support live?+

In the #korvus-indicator channel: the checkout buttons live right in the channel, build previews and release news land there first, and delivery and setup are handled directly with the desk.

Can I just lurk?+

Absolutely. Read the floor as long as you like and speak when you have something. Nobody gets called out for watching.

What is Server AI?+

The house assistant in #server-ai, run on the BlackCrown Intelligence engine. Every member gets 10 questions a week, resetting Monday. It answers on trading concepts, ecommerce and AI, and never gives financial advice.

How do I know a link is really from BlackCrown?+

Check #blackcrown-links. It is the official index of everything the company ships, kept current by the company itself. If a link is not listed there, it did not come from us.
