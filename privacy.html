Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BlackCrownVxJ LLC ("BlackCrown," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our websites and services, including Korvus Industries, BlackCrown Intelligence, and the Eclipse-X community (together, the "Services").

01Information we collect

We collect information in a few ways:

  • Information you provide. Account details such as your name and email, messages you send us, and content you enter into the Services.
  • Payment information. When you subscribe, our payment processor handles your card details. We receive limited billing information such as confirmation of payment and the last digits of your card, not your full card number.
  • Usage information. Information about how you use the Services, such as pages viewed, features used, device and browser type, and approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies. Small files that help the Services work, remember your preferences, and measure performance.

02How we use information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Services.
  • To create and manage your account and process subscriptions and payments.
  • To respond to your messages and provide support.
  • To keep the Services secure and prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To understand how the Services are used and to develop new features.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

03How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to run the Services:

  • Service providers. Trusted vendors that host our infrastructure, process payments, and provide communication and analytics, who may handle information on our behalf.
  • Eclipse-X. If you join our community, information you post there is visible to other members and is handled under the terms of the platform we use to host it.
  • Legal and safety. When required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of BlackCrown, our users, or the public.
  • Business transfers. In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

04Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Services and to measure usage so we can improve them. You can control cookies through your browser settings. Some features may not work properly if you disable them.

05Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Services, comply with our legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we take steps to delete or de-identify it.

06Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07Your choices and rights

You can review and update your account information, unsubscribe from marketing messages, and control cookies. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of your personal information. To make a request, contact us at BlackCrownVxJ.LLC@outlook.com.

08Children

The Services are not directed to children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

09Third-party sites

The Services may link to sites and services we do not control. Their privacy practices are their own, and we encourage you to review their policies.

10Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we will update the date above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice.

11Contact

Questions about this Policy or your information can be sent to BlackCrownVxJ.LLC@outlook.com or (703) 298-9426.
