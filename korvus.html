What you get.

Four tools share one terminal. Everything marked Operator is part of the paid plan; the rest is free.

Scored news from three desks

Asia, London and New York are read around the clock across a 59-instrument universe. Each card carries a clean title, the key numbers, why it matters and a badge for the desk it came from.

SEC filings at the source

Korvus checks EDGAR every 20 seconds for 8-K and 6-K filings from the Nasdaq and S&P heavyweights and reads the release itself, often ahead of the headlines. Operators see it at once, Observers about 15 minutes later.

Impact, direction and confidence

Every item is rated high, medium or low impact and tagged bullish, bearish or neutral with a confidence read, so you see what it means before you read a word of the story.

SMT divergence

NQ, ES and YM are checked against each other at session extremes. Korvus flags when the indexes stop agreeing, which often shows up before a turn.

Session map and prior levels

A global clock tracks Asia, London and New York, with completed session highs and lows and the prior day and week kept on the board.

Macro and the economic calendar

The dollar, the 10 year, gold, oil, credit and volatility sit in one panel, next to a calendar that fills in each actual as it prints.

Compass Operator

A separate board for buy-and-hold investing. About 650 companies scored from 0 to 100 on trend, growth, profit, value and balance sheet, each with a plain-English brief. Research, not advice.

Real-time quotes and alerts Operator

Live pricing through US market hours, personal watchlist imports, and an email the moment a high-impact story lands. Observers get the default board with quotes on a 15-minute delay.

Is Korvus right for you?

We would rather you know before you sign up.

A good fit if

  • You trade index futures such as NQ, ES or YM, or the micros
  • News regularly moves price before you have seen it
  • You want context and reasons, and you make your own decisions
  • You want one screen for news, sessions, macro and the calendar

Probably not for you if

  • You want entry signals, alerts to buy or sell, or a bot that trades for you
  • You need a broker or order execution. Korvus does not place trades
  • You want a promise of profit. Nobody honest can give you one

Questions.

Not answered here? Ask the desk.

What exactly is Korvus?

A web terminal at korvus.industries that reads and scores the news and SEC filings 24/7 so you trade with context. It is not a broker and it does not place trades. It is the information layer under your decisions.

Where does the news come from?

Three desks that follow the sun: Asia, London and New York newswires and central banks, plus 8-K and 6-K filings read straight from SEC EDGAR, checked every 20 seconds on business days. Every card is tagged with the desk it came from, and the source list changes as feeds come and go.

What does Observer get me for free?

The full scored news feed from all three desks, SEC filing reads on a short delay, SMT divergence during market hours, the session map, the macro panel, the economic calendar with actuals, and the default funds board with quotes on a 15-minute delay. No card required.

What does Operator add?

For $9.99 a month or $79.99 a year: SEC filings the moment they are accepted, the Compass long-term investing board, real-time quotes during market hours, personal watchlist imports, high-impact email alerts, and the LodeStone TradingView indicator. It renews until you cancel.

What is Compass?

A separate board inside Korvus for buy-and-hold investing, included with Operator. About 650 companies are scored from 0 to 100 on trend, growth, profit, value and balance sheet, each with a plain-English brief. The scores are research summaries of public data, not recommendations.

I am new to futures. Where do I start?

Korvus publishes free plain-English guides on sessions, margin, tick value, rollover, SMT divergence and more. Start with the beginner guide to futures trading.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. You can delete it yourself from your account page on korvus.industries once any paid subscription has been cancelled.

Is this financial advice?

No. Korvus is an information tool. It tells you what happened and how hard it can hit, it never gives trade signals, and the decision stays yours. Trading futures carries substantial risk of loss.
