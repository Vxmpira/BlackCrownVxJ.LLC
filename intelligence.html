Questions.

Not answered here? Ask the desk.

What is BlackCrown Intelligence versus Crown?

BlackCrown Intelligence is the AI division of BlackCrownVxJ LLC: the team and the engine. Crown is the division's flagship product, the private workspace you log into at blackcrown-intelligence.com.

Is my data used to train models?

Never. Nothing you write, upload or track in Crown is sold or used to train a model. Privacy is the reason the product exists.

What does the Free tier include?

The Crown console with a monthly allowance that refreshes every 30 days, and the full privacy standard. No card and no trial clock.

What does Crowned add?

For $49.99 a month: all four rooms, $100 of room credit, live web access with cited sources, image generation, file uploads and Projects. Deep-Logic draws from your credit balance, and top-ups from $4.99 never expire.

Why does Deep-Logic cost more per answer?

Because it thinks harder. The reasoning room spends more compute on each answer, and Crown passes that through honestly instead of hiding it behind a vague limit.

Why choose Crown over the big names?

Similar frontier intelligence is available in many places. The difference is what is built around it. Crown is built for ownership: your work is not the product, the price is flat, and the balance is visible.
